CS 549: statics and grasp selection, demonstrations

Interactive demonstrations for the statics and grasp selection slides. They run in the browser; nothing is installed. They are made for a laptop or a tablet in landscape (at least about 1024 pixels wide).

Interactive

  • Forces and wrenches
  • Friction cones
  • Force allocation
  • Antipodal grasps: surface normals
  • Antipodal grasps: candidate pairs
  • Perception to grasp: target selection
  • Perception to grasp: observed and inferred contacts

Recorded Drake simulations

  • Pushed box: low push (sliding)
  • Pushed box: high push (tipping)
  • Inclined ramp: tips first
  • Grasp stress test: side load +y
  • Grasp stress test: down load −z
  • Grasp stress test: twist 0.15 N m
  • Grasp stress test: twist 0.25 N m
